# Lemonade: 12 Days of Summer

**[PLAY IN BROWSER — V0.15.4](https://aidan600.github.io/lemonade-15-days-of-summer/)**

Choose what to sell, set prices and compete against Sally over twelve days.
V0.15.4 gives Loyalty Cards deliberate return visits to the stand that earned
its Regulars and makes those relationships harder for Sally to win over.
Day 1 is Cloudy or Sunny. Laid-back Sally usually keeps her mix and prices for
two selling days. Standard starting choices use a validated exact lookup.
The approved introduction, its optional preference and street speed controls remain.

Existing artwork, Summer Records and replay timing remain. Godot 4.7.2 stable,
Compatibility renderer, existing 60 FPS ceiling and VSync. Loyalty effects and
opening weather intentionally change economic outcomes; no win rate is enforced.
