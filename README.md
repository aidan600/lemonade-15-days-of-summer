# Lemonade: 12 Days of Summer

**[PLAY IN BROWSER — V0.15.2](https://aidan600.github.io/lemonade-15-days-of-summer/)**

Choose what to sell, set prices and compete against Sally over twelve days.
V0.15.2 makes morning planning clearer and street decisions easier to follow.
Day 1 starts with no pitchers selected; choose a production plan to open.
Playable cards retain their selection and deselection controls, with no separate
TODAY'S BONUS status area. Street replay shows every paying customer with fewer
low-information passersby, daily effects and a bottom-right Skip/View Results action.
Economic outcomes, Sally, random streams and all three playback speeds are unchanged.

The approved landing page, artwork and Summer Records remain intact.
Windows records persist locally; Web records reset on reload.
Built with Godot 4.7.2 stable, Compatibility renderer, with the existing 60 FPS cap.
